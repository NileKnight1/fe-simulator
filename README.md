## From Heart To Heart
- A game made in Godot for Jackpot - Hackclub

### Controls
- WASD for moving.
- Shift for sprinting.

### Story
- The game starts where you meet a girl.
- You ask AI how to make her like you.
- You talk with a scientist to help you.
- He helps you to enter your body.
- You play as RBC.
- You saerch for oxygens.
- You try to survive in the body .. but macrophages catch you.
- You play as an Fe atom.
- You enter the girl's body.
- You have to survive as a shiny Fe.
- You search for hemoglobin to be an RBC.
- You win!

  If your're stuck in a level tell me to make it easier.
